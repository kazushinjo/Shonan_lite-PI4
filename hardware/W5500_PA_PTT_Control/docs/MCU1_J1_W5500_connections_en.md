# MCU1 / J1 / W5500 (A1) Connection List

English translation of [`MCU1_J1_W5500_接続一覧.md`](MCU1_J1_W5500_接続一覧.md) (Japanese original).
If the two differ, the Japanese original takes precedence.

A mapping table between MCU1 (ESP32 DevKitC, reference display only, DNP), J1 (the actual DevKitC socket) and A1
(W5500_Lite), extracted from the netlist of the KiCad schematic (`hardware/W5500_PA_PTT_Control/kicad/w5500-esp32.kicad_sch`).

MCU1 is a reference-display symbol on the schematic and is DNP (not populated). The actual part is the Freenove
DevKitC board plugged into J1, so the SPI signals (CS/SCK/MISO/MOSI) have separate net names (labels only) on the MCU1
side, and the actual wiring is direct wires between J1 and A1.

On 2026-08-30, J1 was corrected from 2x19 (38 pins) to 2x20 (40 pins) to match the actual pinout of the real board
(Freenove ESP32-WROOM-32E DevKitC). Furthermore, on 2026-08-31 MCU1 was also replaced with the same
`project_symbols:ESP32_DevKitC_Socket_40P` symbol as J1, so **MCU1's pin numbers and signal names now match J1
exactly** (previously MCU1 used the pad numbers of the ESP32-WROOM-32 module itself (KiCad standard
`RF_Module:ESP32-WROOM-32` library), which did not match J1's socket pin numbers). The pin numbers in the table below
are common to MCU1 and J1.

| Signal | MCU1/J1 pin | W5500 (A1) pin | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPI CS | GPIO5 (31) | CS (5) | Separate net name on the MCU1 side (label only); actual wiring is between J1 and A1 |
| SPI SCK | GPIO18 (32) | SCK (4) | Same as above |
| SPI MISO | GPIO19 (33) | MISO (12) | Same as above |
| SPI MOSI | GPIO23 (39) | MOSI (3) | Same as above |
| W5500 RST | GPIO21 (35) | RST (11) | Same net for all three (GPIO-controlled reset; the firmware sends a pulse at startup). Pulled up to +3V3_A by R9 (10 kΩ) |
| POWER (12 V power) control | GPIO26 (11) | — | Not connected to the W5500. The net label is `GPIO26_PA`, but it actually controls the 12 V power ON/OFF (see the table below) |
| PTT control | GPIO27 (10) | — | Not connected to the W5500. The net label is `GPIO27_PTT`. Pulls the radio's PTT terminal to GND (see the table below) |
| GND | GND (7) | 1, 2, 7 | Common GND. J1 and MCU1 also have GND pins at 21–24, 34 and 40, which are unconnected |
| +5 V (`+5v0`, DevKitC board supply) | 5V (1, 2) | — | Supplied from U2 (L7805, +12 V → +5 V). MCU1 (the ESP32 module itself) runs on 3.3 V and has no corresponding pin. The on-board LDO of the DevKitC board converts this 5 V to 3.3 V for the ESP32 module |
| +3.3 V (ESP32 side) | 3.3V (3) | — | Output of the DevKitC board's on-board LDO (generated from the +5 V above). The supply for the VDD pin of MCU1 (the ESP32 module). J1 and MCU1 also have 3.3 V pins at 4, 5 and 20, which are unconnected |
| +3V3_A (W5500 only) | — | VCC (8, 9) | Generated by U1 (TA48033S, +5 V → +3.3 V) from the same `+5v0` (U2 output) as above. The generating circuit is separate from the ESP32 side's 3.3 V (via the DevKitC board's built-in LDO), but the source 5 V is common |

Unused: A1's INT (6) and NC (10), and the remaining GPIO pins of J1/MCU1 (GPIO13, GPIO12, GPIO14, GPIO33, GPIO32,
GPIO35, GPIO34, VN, VP, EN, GPIO15, GPIO2, GPIO0, GPIO4, GPIO16, GPIO17, RX, TX, GPIO22) are not connected this time.

> The MCU1 symbol is "reference display only, DNP" on the schematic and represents the Freenove DevKitC board itself
> plugged into J1. Since 2026-08-31 it uses the same `project_symbols:ESP32_DevKitC_Socket_40P` symbol as J1, so the
> ESP32-module-specific flash pins (SD0–SD3/CMD/CLK, etc.) that the DevKitC board itself does not have are not shown.
>
> The constraint that "the ESP32 module chip itself runs on 3.3 V and has no 5 V input pin, so there is no corresponding
> MCU1 pin for the +5 V system" applied to the old symbol. With the current DevKitC socket display, the "5V" pin is also
> shown (as the power the DevKitC board actually receives), but it remains unconnected in this circuit. What actually
> receives the +5 V is the Freenove DevKitC board plugged into J1, and the 5 V → 3.3 V conversion by its on-board LDO is
> outside the scope of this schematic (inside the DevKitC board).
>
> The +3V3_A for the W5500 (A1) is generated by U1 (TA48033S) further stepping down to 3.3 V **the same `+5v0` net**
> (U2 = L7805 output) as the +5 V for MCU1 (DevKitC board). In other words, the 12 V → 5 V step-down is a single system
> through U2 (L7805), which branches in two directions from there, for MCU1 and for the W5500 (no external DC-DC buck
> converter module is used).

GPIO25 (J1/MCU1 pin 12) was labeled as the LNA control net (`GPIO25_LNA`) as of Rev.1.1, but it is not connected to any
drive circuit in this table or the table below and is unused in the current circuit (outside the scope of the
PA_Power/PTT controller).

## POWER (12 V Power) and PTT Drive Circuit Connection Details

GPIO26 and GPIO27 are connected to J5 (Power) and J6 (PTT_ON) through the following drive circuits, independently of
the W5500.

| GPIO | Path | Parts | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| GPIO26 (POWER) | GPIO26 → R6 → Q1 (B), pulled down to GND by R12 | R6: 510 Ω, R12: 10 kΩ, Q1: 2SC1815 (NPN) | When GPIO26 is HIGH, Q1 turns ON and pulls Q5's gate toward GND. While GPIO26 is undetermined, R12 keeps Q1 OFF |
| ↳ | Q1 (C) → Q5 (G), pulled up by R8 | R8: 1 kΩ (pull-up to +12 V, 1/2 W recommended), Q5: 2SJ334 (PMOS, TO-220, pins 1 = G, 2 = D, 3 = S, with heatsink) | When Q1 is OFF, R8 pulls the gate up to +12 V and Q5 (high-side switch) is OFF |
| ↳ | Q5 (S) = +12 V, Q5 (D) → J5 (Power) | J5: Power output connector | When Q5 is ON, +12 V conducts to J5 (Power) (replaces the former relay K3) |
| GPIO27 (PTT) | GPIO27 → R7 → Q3 (B) | R7: 510 Ω, Q3: 2SC1815 (NPN) | When GPIO27 is HIGH, Q3 turns ON |
| ↳ | Q3 (C) → J6 (PTT_ON) | — | When Q3 (acting like an open collector) is ON, it pulls J6 (PTT_ON) to GND (intended for connection to the radio's PTT terminal; high impedance when Q3 is OFF) |

The emitters (pin 1) of Q1 and Q3 are connected to the common GND.

## Power System Connection Details

Starting from the externally supplied +12 V (13.8 V), **the +5 V (`+5v0`) generated by U2 (L7805) is branched to both
MCU1 (DevKitC board) and U1 (TA48033S)**. The 12 V → 5 V step-down is a single system through U2, and no external DC-DC
buck converter module is used.

| Path | Parts | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Power input: J2 (DC_IN_13V8) → +12 V net | J2: 2 pins (+12 V, GND) | Input connector for the external power supply (13.8 V) |
| +12 V → U2 (IN) → U2 (OUT) = `+5v0` | U2: L7805 (TO-220, 12 V → 5 V) | C3 (0.1 µF) bypasses +12 V to GND |
| `+5v0` → J1 (1, 2) | — | Supplies MCU1 (DevKitC board). Converted to 3.3 V by the on-board LDO and passed to the ESP32 module |
| `+5v0` → U1 (IN) → U1 (OUT) = `+3V3_A` → A1 (VCC) | U1: TA48033S (TO-220, 5 V → 3.3 V) | C1 (0.1 µF) bypasses `+5v0` to GND, C2 (0.1 µF) bypasses `+3V3_A` to GND |

Note that `+5v0` (U2 output) is the **common supply** for J1 (for MCU1) and U1 (input of the 3.3 V regulator for the
W5500), and the two branch in parallel from the same net (as of Rev.2.0, J3 for an external DC-DC buck converter
existed as a separate system, but it was removed in Rev.2.1 on 2026-08-30 and merged into the single system from U2).
